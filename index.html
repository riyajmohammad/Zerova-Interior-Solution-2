GOOD TO KNOW

A few things
you might wonder.

Which cities do you serve?

We serve Lucknow, Kanpur, Sitapur, Barabanki, Unnao and Raebareli. Get in touch to ask about your project location.

How do I get an accurate project quote?

Send us your chosen service, city and a few project details using the enquiry form. We’ll discuss the scope and confirm pricing with you directly.

Are the prices on this page final?

The listed panel, ceiling and sheet rates are indicative. Tiles, ACP, wooden and electrician work are quoted according to the deal. Final pricing depends on material, quantity and project scope; please confirm with our team.

How does the booking form work?

The form prepares a WhatsApp message with the details you enter. WhatsApp opens so you can review and send your enquiry to our team. Your information is not stored on this website.
